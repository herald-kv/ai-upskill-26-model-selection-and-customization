# BrightCart Customer Support Handbook

Version 7.2. Internal use only. BrightCart, its policies, people and numbers are fictional and exist only for training.

This handbook is the single source of truth for BrightCart support agents, human and AI. When a customer question is covered here, answer from the handbook and name the section you used. When it is not covered, say so and escalate. Never invent a policy, a fee, a date or a promise.

## 1. Who we are and how we talk

BrightCart is an online marketplace selling electronics, home appliances, fashion and groceries across India. We ship from 14 fulfilment centres and serve about 3 million active customers a month.

### 1.1 Tone of voice

- Warm, plain and brief. Use the customer's first name once, at the start.
- Lead with the answer. The explanation comes second.
- No jargon. Say "refund to your card" rather than "reversal to the source instrument".
- Never blame the customer, the courier or another team. Say what we will do.
- Apologise once, sincerely, when we got something wrong. Don't apologise repeatedly.
- Don't use exclamation marks more than once per reply. Never use emoji in billing or security replies.

### 1.2 Reply structure

1. One sentence that answers the question or states the outcome.
2. The steps the customer needs to take, numbered, if any.
3. What happens next and when, with a realistic time frame from this handbook.
4. The ticket reference.

### 1.3 Languages

We answer in the language the customer wrote in, if it is English, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Telugu, Bengali or Marathi. For any other language, answer in English and offer a callback with an interpreter.

## 2. Orders

### 2.1 Order statuses

| Status | Meaning | Customer can cancel? |
|---|---|---|
| Placed | Payment received, not yet packed | Yes, free |
| Packed | At the fulfilment centre, label printed | Yes, free |
| Shipped | Handed to the courier | No, but can refuse delivery |
| Out for delivery | With the delivery agent today | No, but can refuse delivery |
| Delivered | Courier marked it delivered | No, use returns |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by the customer or by us | Not applicable |

### 2.2 Cancelling an order

Customers can cancel from the app under My Orders while the order is Placed or Packed. There is no fee. The refund starts the moment the order is cancelled; see section 4.3 for how long it takes to arrive.

If the order is Shipped or Out for delivery, the customer can refuse it at the door. The courier brings it back, and the refund starts once the fulfilment centre scans it, normally 3 to 5 working days after refusal. Cash-on-delivery orders that are refused have nothing to refund.

Grocery orders can be cancelled only until they are Packed, because groceries are packed within 2 hours of a delivery slot.

### 2.3 Changing an order

The delivery address can be changed while the order is Placed, to another address in the same pincode zone. Items, sizes and colours can't be changed after the order is placed. The customer cancels and orders again. Any coupon used is returned to the account within 24 hours if it has not expired.

### 2.4 Split shipments

One order can arrive in several parcels from different fulfilment centres. Each parcel has its own tracking number under the same order. Tell the customer this before treating a missing item as lost: check whether the other parcels are still on their way.

## 3. Delivery

### 3.1 Delivery times

| Area | Standard | Express (BrightCart Plus members) |
|---|---|---|
| Metro cities | 2 to 3 days | Next day |
| Tier 2 cities | 3 to 5 days | 2 days |
| Rest of India | 5 to 8 days | Not available |
| Groceries (40 cities) | Chosen 2-hour slot | Chosen 2-hour slot |

Large appliances (fridges, washing machines, TVs above 43 inches, air conditioners) add 2 days to these times and are delivered by our specialist partner, HeavyHaul, with a scheduled slot.

### 3.2 Delivery charges

Standard delivery is free on orders above Rs 499. Below that it costs Rs 40. Express delivery is free for BrightCart Plus members and not offered to others. Large appliances are always delivered free, and installation is free for air conditioners and washing machines within 48 hours of delivery.

### 3.3 Marked delivered but not received

1. Ask the customer to check with neighbours, building security and any other family members, and to look for a delivery photo in the app.
2. If the parcel is still missing after 24 hours, raise a Delivery Investigation (DI) ticket. The courier has 48 hours to respond.
3. If the courier cannot show a delivery photo or a signature, or doesn't respond in 48 hours, refund the customer in full, or send a free replacement if the item is in stock. The customer chooses.
4. A customer may receive at most 2 refunds for non-delivery in 12 months without a manual review by the Trust team. From the third claim onwards, raise the DI and tell the customer the review takes up to 5 working days.

### 3.4 Damaged on arrival

Customers should photograph the outer box and the item and report damage within 48 hours of delivery (7 days for large appliances, because they are often unboxed by the installer).

- Small items: we send a free replacement, or refund if the customer prefers. The customer does not need to return the damaged item if its value is below Rs 1,000.
- Large appliances: HeavyHaul or the brand's technician inspects within 3 working days. If the damage happened in transit, we replace the appliance. Customers may ask for a replacement instead of a repair when the damage happened in transit; repairs are only offered for faults that appear later and are covered by the brand warranty.
- If the courier disputes the damage, the customer's photos and the inspection report decide it. The customer should never have to argue with the courier directly. We do that.

### 3.5 Delays

If an order is more than 3 days late against the promised date, we apologise and offer a Rs 100 voucher (Rs 250 for BrightCart Plus members). If it is more than 10 days late, the customer may cancel for a full refund even if it has shipped.

## 4. Payments, refunds and billing

### 4.1 Payment methods

We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, BrightCart Wallet, EMI on cards from 11 banks, and cash on delivery (orders up to Rs 50,000, not for gift cards or groceries above Rs 5,000).

### 4.2 Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge is when the same order is paid for twice. It usually happens when the customer retries a payment that seemed to fail.

1. Confirm in the payments console that two successful captures exist for one order.
2. If both are successful, refund the second capture straight away. No approval is needed.
3. If the second one shows "pending", tell the customer it will be reversed by their bank automatically within 5 working days, and that no action is needed.

### 4.3 Refund times

| Original payment method | Time after refund starts |
|---|---|
| BrightCart Wallet | Instant |
| UPI | 1 to 3 working days |
| Debit or credit card | 5 to 7 working days |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 working days |
| EMI | 7 to 10 working days; the bank cancels the remaining instalments |
| Cash on delivery | To the BrightCart Wallet instantly, or to a bank account in 3 to 5 working days |

Customers can always choose an instant refund to the BrightCart Wallet instead.

### 4.4 Coupons, store credit and combo offers

- A coupon used on an order that is cancelled before it ships is returned to the account within 24 hours if it has not expired. A coupon used on an order that is returned is not given back.
- Store credit from an exchange or goodwill gesture is kept in the BrightCart Wallet for 12 months. It does not disappear when another order is refunded. If a customer says it has, it is almost always the expiry or a display bug; check the wallet ledger and restore it if it is missing.
- Combo offers: if a customer returns part of a combo, the refund for that item is its share of the combo price, worked out in proportion to the items' list prices. The remaining items keep their discount. The customer doesn't have to return the whole combo.

### 4.5 GST and business invoices

Business customers on BrightCart Business can add a GSTIN to their account. Invoices then show the GSTIN and the tax breakdown. Items under the GST exempt list (fresh produce, some books and some medical supplies) must show 0% GST. If a business invoice shows GST on an exempt item, raise a Billing Correction ticket to the Finance team; they issue a corrected invoice within 3 working days. Support agents cannot edit invoices.

### 4.6 Bulk pricing tiers

BrightCart Business accounts with 25 or more seats see bulk pricing tiers. Visibility is controlled by the account's plan and each seat's role (Buyer, Approver, Admin). Only Buyers and Admins see bulk prices. After a product update, if some seats cannot see bulk tiers, check their roles first, then raise a Technical ticket with the account ID and the affected seat emails.

## 5. Returns and exchanges

### 5.1 Return windows

| Category | Window | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets) | 10 days | Unused, all accessories, original box. Opened box is accepted. |
| Large appliances | 10 days | Only for defects or damage, not change of mind |
| Fashion and footwear | 30 days | Unworn, tags attached |
| Home and kitchen | 15 days | Unused |
| Groceries | No returns | Refund or replacement only for damaged, expired or wrong items, reported within 48 hours |
| Gift cards, software keys, personal care | Not returnable | |

Days are counted from the delivery date. The window for a laptop is 10 days; a laptop bought 20 days ago is outside the window, but it may still be covered by the brand warranty if it is faulty. Offer to help the customer contact the brand service centre.

### 5.2 How returns work

1. The customer starts the return in the app under My Orders and picks a reason.
2. A pickup is scheduled within 2 working days. Customers in pincodes without pickup are given a self-ship label and reimbursed up to Rs 150.
3. The item is checked at pickup (fashion, home) or at the fulfilment centre (electronics).
4. The refund starts once the check passes. See 4.3 for times.

### 5.3 Exchanges

Fashion and footwear can be exchanged once for a different size or colour of the same product, if it is in stock. Exchanges are free. Everything else is returned and reordered.

## 6. Accounts and security

### 6.1 Changing account details

- Email address: Account > Profile > Email. A code is sent to both the old and the new address. If the customer can no longer access the old email, verify identity with the phone number and the last order number, then make the change from the agent console.
- Phone number: Account > Profile > Phone. Requires an OTP on the new number and the account password.
- Name and addresses can be changed at any time from the profile.

### 6.2 Suspected account takeover

Treat any report of unrecognised logins or orders as urgent.

1. Lock the account from the agent console. This logs out every session and freezes the BrightCart Wallet.
2. Cancel any unrecognised orders that have not shipped. For shipped ones, raise a Fraud Intercept so the courier returns them.
3. Remove all saved cards and UPI IDs from the account, and tell the customer to contact their bank to block the card that was used.
4. Raise a Security Incident ticket to the Trust team, who will contact the customer within 24 hours.
5. Refund fraudulent orders in full once the Trust team confirms the case, normally within 5 working days.
6. Ask the customer to turn on two-step verification when the account is unlocked.

Changing the password alone is not enough, because the attacker may still have an active session or saved payment methods.

### 6.3 Deleting an account

Customers can ask for their account and personal data to be deleted under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

1. Confirm the request with an OTP to the registered phone number.
2. Tell the customer that open orders, returns and refunds must finish first, that the wallet balance will be lost unless it is withdrawn, and that invoices are kept for 8 years because tax law requires it.
3. Raise a Data Deletion request. The account is deleted within 30 days and the customer receives a confirmation email.

## 7. Technical issues

### 7.1 Before escalating

Ask for the device, operating system, app version and a screenshot or screen recording. Suggest, in this order: update the app, clear the app cache, reinstall, and try the website.

### 7.2 Known issues (updated weekly)

| Issue | Workaround | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Cart crashes on Android 15 with app version 9.4.0 | Update to 9.4.2 | Fixed in 9.4.2 |
| UPI payment page spins on some banks | Use the UPI ID option instead of the QR, or pay by card | Fix in progress, ETA next release |
| Order tracking map blank on iOS 18.1 | Use the text tracking view | Investigating |
| Bulk pricing not visible to some Business seats after the 24 September update | Ask an Admin to re-save the seat's role | Fix in progress |

### 7.3 Escalating

Raise a Technical ticket with the device details, steps to reproduce and the customer's account ID. Engineering triages Technical tickets within 1 working day. Never promise a fix date that is not in the known issues table.

## 8. BrightCart Plus

BrightCart Plus costs Rs 1,499 a year or Rs 179 a month. Benefits: free express delivery, early access to sales, 5% back in the wallet on electronics, and priority support. Members can cancel at any time; annual plans are refunded pro rata for unused full months, monthly plans are not refunded for the current month.

|---|---|---|

| Corrected invoices, GST issues | Finance | 3 working days |
| App and website bugs | Engineering (Technical ticket) | 1 working day |
| Damaged large appliances, courier disputes | Logistics | 3 working days |
| Legal threats, media, regulators | Escalations Manager | Same day |
| Anything not covered in this handbook | Team Lead | Same day |

## 10. What AI agents must never do

- Promise a refund, replacement or compensation that this handbook does not allow.
- Share another customer's information, or confirm whether an email or phone number has an account.
- Ask for a full card number, CVV, OTP or password. Ever.
- Give legal, medical or financial advice.
- Close a ticket about safety (fire, electric shock, injury). Escalate it to the Escalations Manager immediately.
